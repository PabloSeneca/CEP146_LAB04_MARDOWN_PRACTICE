# Eclair Recipe

## Pastry Cream Filling
- 4 large egg yolks, cold or room temperature
- 3 Tablespoons (23g) cornstarch
- 2 cups (480ml) whole milk (do not use low-fat or nondairy)
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 Tablespoon (14g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
 -seeds scraped from 1/2 vanilla bean (or extra 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract)
 -small pinch salt

## Choux Pastry
- 1 batch choux pastry
- egg wash: 1 egg beaten with 1 Tablespoon milk or water
  
## Chocolate Ganache Topping
- 1/2 cup (120ml) heavy cream
- 4 ounces (113g) semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. **Make the pastry cream:** Whisk egg yolks and cornstarch until combined. If too dry, add a few drops of the milk from the next step. Set aside. Heat milk and sugar in a saucepan until the sugar dissolves and the mixture gently simmers. Slowly whisk the hot milk into the egg mixture to temper it. Strain back into the saucepan, then whisk constantly over medium heat until thick and bubbling, about 1–2 minutes. Let the bubbles burst for about 20 seconds (185–190°F / 85–88°C). Remove from heat and whisk in butter, vanilla, vanilla seeds, and salt. Chill for at least 3 hours, up to 24 hours.
2. Make the choux pastry through step 4 of the recipe. **Use immediately or refrigerate**, covered, for up to 3 days.
3. Heat oven to 400°F (204°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment and lightly brush with water.
4. Transfer dough to a piping bag **fitted with a large tip**. Pipe 4-inch logs about 3 inches apart. Smooth any peaks with a damp finger and brush lightly with egg wash.
5. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes. **Without opening the oven**, reduce to 350°F (177°C) and bake another 8–12 minutes, until golden. Cool completely on a rack.
6. **Make the ganache:** Place chopped chocolate in a bowl. Heat cream until gently simmering, then pour over chocolate. Let sit 2–3 minutes, then stir until smooth. Cool for about 15 minutes to thicken.
7. Transfer chilled pastry cream to a piping bag with a small round or star tip. Poke a hole in each eclair and pipe in the filling, using both ends if needed. Alternatively, slice the eclairs horizontally and fill. Dip the tops in ganache and let set for about 1 hour at room temperature or 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
